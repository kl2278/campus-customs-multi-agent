Campus Customs: Desk Tickets Plan

My plan for the 3 open tickets, written before running any agents. Shop date: 2026-08-31. Checking balance: $3,400.

Ticket 101: one white tee, size S

Ticket facts

Request
One white tee (CC-TEE-WHITE), size S.
Stock
0 on hand, Aisle B. Out of stock.
Linked invoice
Invoice 501: $840.00 from vendor 1 (apparel reprint, 5-day lead time), due 2026-08-28, still open.
Overdue?
Yes, 3 days overdue against the shop date 2026-08-31.
Vendor rule
Vendor 1 cannot ship while invoice 501 is open.

Expected

Boss calls first, and why

Inventory. Everything else depends on whether we can restock and who can ship, and Inventory is the agent that checks stock and vendors.

Delegations I expect

Boss to Inventory. Inventory finds the shortfall, picks vendor 1 by specialty (apparel reprint, since the database has no SKU-to-vendor link), and sees that vendor 1 can't ship because invoice 501 is open. Inventory (or Boss) then delegates to Accounting to queue the $840 payment for human approval. Boss then delegates to Customer Service to draft a status message for the customer. Facilities is not involved because nothing about the lease or space is touched.

MCP tools I expect this run to use

get_ticket get_shop_date check_stock list_vendors get_vendor_ship_status get_invoice (or list_open_invoices) get_cash_balance list_approvals queue_payment_for_approval save_customer_draft

Expected end state

The payment is pending human approval. The agents can't approve it, so the purchase order can't be created until the payment is approved and executed. If it were created today, it would arrive 5 days after the shop date (2026-09-05). The customer draft should say the restock is pending and should not promise a date as certain.

Actual

Filled in from the audit trail and the database after the real runs.

Who the Boss called first

Inventory, in both working runs. The very first attempt (run 534ea9de3a8d) got no answer at all: the gateway rejected the Boss's first request, so nothing was called.

Delegations

  • Run 2 (b2863114f44b): Boss to Inventory; Inventory to Accounting (queued the $840 payment for approval); Boss to Accounting (re-read the invoice and approval); Boss to Customer Service (saved draft 1).
  • Run 3 (814ef7f0c0a9), after a human approved the payment: Boss to Inventory (created purchase order 1); Boss to Accounting (confirmed the invoice was paid); Boss to Customer Service (saved draft 2).
  • Facilities was not involved.

Tools used

get_ticket ×7 list_approvals ×5 list_purchase_orders ×4 list_customer_drafts ×4 get_invoice ×3 check_stock ×2 list_vendors ×2 get_vendor_ship_status ×2 list_payments ×2 get_shop_date ×2 save_customer_draft ×2 get_cash_balance ×1 queue_payment_for_approval ×1 create_purchase_order ×1

Counts are across all three runs. Accounting never called execute_approved_payment: the approve route executes payments.

Outcome

Resolved. The $840 invoice 501 payment was approved by a human and executed (cash $3,400.00 to $2,560.00). Purchase order 1 (one tee, $8.00, expected 2026-09-05) was recorded and approved; it moved no cash. Two customer drafts are saved and unsent.

How it differed from my plan

  • First call: matched (Inventory).
  • Delegations: matched. Inventory handed the payment to Accounting, Customer Service drafted a message, and Facilities was not involved. Extra: the Boss also delegated to Accounting itself and re-read the approvals, orders and drafts, and a second working run was needed after the payment was approved.
  • Tools: all ten tools I listed were used (list_open_invoices was not, which my plan allowed). Not in my plan: list_purchase_orders, list_customer_drafts, list_payments and create_purchase_order.
  • End state: matched for the first working run (payment pending approval, no purchase order, draft 1 gave no date). After the payment was approved, the purchase order was created as I expected, arriving 5 days after the shop date (2026-09-05), approved with no cash change. Not matched: draft 2 mentioned a pending purchase order and gave an estimated arrival date, which breaks the Customer Service rule. Also not in my plan: the first attempt failed at the gateway before any work.

The rent payment is pending human approval. $3,400 covers $2,400, but paying both this and invoice 501 leaves $160.

Ticket 103: 20 navy hoodies, size M, bulk discount requested

Ticket facts

Request
20 navy hoodies (CC-HOOD-NAVY), size M, with a bulk discount requested by a student organization.
Stock
8 on hand (Aisle A), so 12 short.
Pricing
Cost $22.00, list $58.00. Margin at list is $36.00 per unit (62.07%).
Restock
Would come from vendor 1 (5-day lead time), which is blocked by the open invoice 501.
Restock cost
12 units at $22 is about $264, more than the $160 left after paying invoice 501 and the rent.

Expected

Boss calls first, and why

Accounting. The core of the ticket is a price override, so the first question is how much discount keeps a safe margin above cost. Stock comes second.

Delegations I expect

Boss to Accounting, who checks pricing and margin at the requested price and checks cash. Then Boss to Inventory, who confirms 8 in stock and a shortfall of 12 and checks vendor 1's ship status (blocked by invoice 501). Then Boss to Customer Service to draft the reply with the Boss's decision. Facilities is not involved.

MCP tools I expect this run to use

get_ticket get_shop_date get_unit_pricing get_cash_balance check_stock list_vendors get_vendor_ship_status list_approvals save_customer_draft

Expected end state

A discount decision that stays above the $22 cost, a draft reply, and no purchase order, because vendor 1 can't ship while invoice 501 is unpaid. Restocking 12 units costs about $264, which doesn't fit in the cash left after paying invoice 501 and the rent ($160), so agents shouldn't queue a second payment for it.

Actual

Filled in from the audit trail and the database after the real runs.

Who the Boss called first

Accounting, in all three runs (39477e8ca8e6, b5fe65e9e187, 94e6c4f1542a).

Delegations

  • Runs 1 and 2: Boss to Accounting (pricing), then Boss to Inventory (stock and vendor).
  • Run 3: Boss to Accounting only. Inventory, Customer Service and Facilities were not called.
  • Customer Service was never called on this ticket, so no draft exists for 103.

Tools used

get_ticket ×7 list_purchase_orders ×4 get_unit_pricing ×3 check_stock ×2 list_vendors ×2 get_vendor_ship_status ×2 create_purchase_order ×2 list_approvals ×2 get_shop_date ×1

Outcome

Resolved in run 3: the Boss approved a unit price of $52.00 (total $1,040.00 for 20), $30.00 above the $22.00 unit cost. Runs 1 and 2 left the discount to a human, and each created a 12-unit purchase order ($264.00 each, PO 2 and PO 3); the human approver rejected both because cash ($160) could not cover $264. The 12-unit shortfall (8 in stock of 20) is not addressed. No cash moved.

How it differed from my plan

  • First call: matched (Accounting).
  • Delegations: runs 1 and 2 followed Accounting then Inventory, but Customer Service was never called. Run 3 skipped Inventory and Customer Service.
  • Tools: used get_ticket, get_shop_date, get_unit_pricing, check_stock, list_vendors, get_vendor_ship_status and list_approvals. Not used: get_cash_balance and save_customer_draft. Not in my plan: list_purchase_orders and create_purchase_order.
  • End state: a discount decision above the $22 cost was reached (matched, in run 3 after the Boss prompt was changed). No payment was queued for the restock (matched). A draft reply was expected and does not exist. No purchase order was expected, but two were created: invoice 501 was already paid when this ticket ran, so vendor 1 could ship, and both orders were rejected. The 12-unit shortfall was left unaddressed.
